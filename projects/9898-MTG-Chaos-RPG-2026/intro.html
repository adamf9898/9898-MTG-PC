When the command to claim your starting inventory is processed, you will receive:

  • 10 Mana Link: Represents your health (your heart) outside of duels and your starting life inside of duels.
  • 150 Gold Coins: Represent a type of currency with many different forms and uses.
  • 2 Haggler's Amulets: Used to restock shops, stores, etc. Two can be used anywhere to restock inventory.
  • 2 Energy: Used to explore when moving on game boards/maps.
  • 6 Chaos Commander Booster Packs: Each contains 2 random common cards per color (white, blue, black, red, green), 2 random uncommon cards, 1 random card, 1 random rare or mythic card, and 1 random legendary creature card.
